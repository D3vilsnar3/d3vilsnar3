A SwiftUI iOS app for logging meals in plain language and getting coached on them, not just tracked. Describe what you ate and the app resolves it to nutrition data, keeps a running log, and hands it to an "Ask" tab for coaching — what to eat next, how a day is trending, whether a goal is realistic.

The part that turns tracking into action: a full partnership with Swiggy and Instamart lets the coaching flow go straight to ordering — food or groceries — from inside the same conversation that just told you what to eat. A serverless AWS backend — Cognito, API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, with Bedrock (Claude) behind the coaching and recipe extraction — holds every credential and runs the AI, so the client app is a thin shell on top of it. A Kotlin/Jetpack Compose Android port now runs on that same backend. 1.0 is submitted for App Store review, with Swiggy production sign-in already live. Full write-up and screens live on its own site — thali.asimovx.com.

  • Natural-language meal logging — no manual macro entry
  • An "Ask" tab that coaches against the running log, not a single meal
  • A full Swiggy and Instamart partnership, ordering straight from the coaching conversation
  • iOS and Android clients on one serverless AWS backend — Cognito, API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, Bedrock
  • Built solo — backend, iOS and Android clients all three
